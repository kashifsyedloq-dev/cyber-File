CASE FILES

Six ways scammers try to get you.

Pick a case to open the simulation. Each one plays out like the real thing — right up until the moment you'd normally lose money, your account, or your files. Then we break character and show you exactly what gave it away.

CASE-01

Phishing Email

A convincing bank alert asks you to "verify" your account before it's suspended.

CASE-02

Fake Job Offer

A recruiter promises a high-paying remote role — for a small "processing fee."

CASE-03

Romance Scam

An online connection builds trust for weeks, then needs urgent financial help.

CASE-04

UPI Fraud

A "refund" request or QR code scan quietly drains your account instead.

CASE-05

Malicious Link

A shortened link in a text or DM leads to a credential-harvesting page.

CASE-06

Ransomware

A disguised attachment locks your files and demands payment to unlock them.
